Get the Leeward Staff app

Install the portal so it opens in its own window from your taskbar, Dock, or home screen, with no browser tabs. Nothing to download from an app store.

Install on this device Follow the steps below for your device.

Microsoft Edge

  1. Open the portal in Edge and sign in.
  2. Click the App available icon on the right side of the address bar. Or open the ··· menu and choose Apps → Install this site as an app.
  3. Click Install.

Google Chrome

  1. Open the portal in Chrome and sign in.
  2. Click the Install icon on the right side of the address bar. Or open the ⋮ menu and choose Cast, save, and share → Install page as app.
  3. Click Install.

Keep it handy

  • Right-click Leeward Staff on the taskbar and choose Pin to taskbar. It’s also in the Start menu.
  • Firefox can’t install apps. Use Edge or Chrome.

Safari

  1. Open the portal in Safari and sign in.
  2. In the menu bar, choose File → Add to Dock.
  3. Click Add.

Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge

  1. Open the portal and sign in.
  2. Click the Install icon on the right side of the address bar.
  3. Click Install.

Keep it handy

  • It shows up in your Dock and in Launchpad. Right-click the Dock icon and choose Options → Keep in Dock so it stays there.
  • Safari’s Add to Dock needs macOS Sonoma or newer. On older Macs, use Chrome or Edge.

Safari

  1. Open the portal in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button in the toolbar.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add in the top corner.

Keep it handy

  • Open the portal from the new Leeward Staff icon on your home screen from now on. It opens full screen, like an app.
  • Turn on notifications from inside the app to get alerts on your phone.
  • Same steps on an iPad.

Google Chrome

  1. Open the portal in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top right.
  3. Tap Install app (on some phones it says Add to Home screen).
  4. Tap Install.

Keep it handy

  • The Leeward Staff icon is added to your home screen and app drawer.
