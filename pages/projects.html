Step 1 of 4

Save project to hard drive

Plug in this project's hard drive

Use a new, empty drive for each project, and label it with the project name. It shows up on your computer as My Passport (or whatever it's named).

Need a drive? Buy a WD My Passport Ultra 2TB (USB-C) ↗

Pick the drive

Click Choose hard drive (last step). A window opens — click My Passport in the left sidebar, then Select (on Windows, Select Folder).

Allow the portal to save to it

Your browser asks "Let site edit files?" — click Edit files (or Allow). The portal can only write inside the drive you picked.

The portal saves everything onto the drive

It makes a folder named after the project with Files (every Project Files folder and file), Records (PDFs of the timeline, contacts, invoices, VPOs, bids, budget and draws) and an Archive summary PDF. Keep this page open until it says it's done, check the folder on the drive, then click I saved it to the hard drive.

This browser can't save straight to a drive. Use Chrome or Edge to skip this step — or download a zip now and drag it onto the drive yourself.
