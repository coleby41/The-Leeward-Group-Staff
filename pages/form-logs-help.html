Form logs

← Back to Form Logs

Form Logs is the record of every VPO, back charge and approved incident report on a project, with their numbers, vendors and amounts.

1Read the log

Open a project and click Form Logs. There's a table for each kind of form, newest first:

  • VPO: variance purchase orders.
  • BC: back charges.
  • IR: approved incident reports.

Click an ID to open the filed PDF. The IR ID column on a VPO or BC links back to the incident report it came from. The VPO table shows the running total at the top.

2Where VPOs and back charges come from

Nothing is typed into Form Logs directly. It all starts with an incident report:

  • Someone fills out an Incident Report (from the sidebar) for a project.
  • The person set as the approver approves or rejects it on Account Activity.
  • Right after approving, they choose: make it a Back Charge (who to charge, who to credit), make it a VPO (pick the vendor), or neither, with a reason.
  • The PDF is filled out and filed in Project Files, and the entry shows up here.
The project needs a code

Numbers like BC-HV-001 are built from the project code. If the project doesn't have one, set it in the project's Edit setup first.

3Delete a VPO or back charge

Use Delete on the row.

Deleting can leave a gap in the numbers

If you delete the newest one, the next form reuses its number. Delete one from the middle and that number is simply skipped. It can't be undone.

4Submit old forms

Submit Old Forms is a temporary tool for bringing in BCs and VPOs from before the portal. Pick a batch of PDFs, check what was read from each one in the table, and create them all at once.

It's going away

Once the old forms are in, this button will be removed.
