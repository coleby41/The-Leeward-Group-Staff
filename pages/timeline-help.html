The project timeline

← Back to Timeline

The Project Timeline is the job's schedule: phases, tasks and milestones on a chart you can drag. The Overview's numbers and the project cards all come from it.

1Read it 2Add work 3Change dates 4Lock the plan 5Delete ?Questions

1Read the timeline

Open a project and click Project Timeline. The list on the left shows each phase with its tasks and milestones; the chart on the right shows when they happen.

  • Switch the chart between Day, Week, Month, Quarter and Year. Today scrolls to today.
  • The tabs across the top (Pre-Construction, Buy Out, Construction, Close-Out) show one kind of phase.
  • Filters narrows it by status, priority, person, contractor, dates, or critical tasks only.
  • A red ⛔ marks a critical task. Diamonds are milestones.
Use a computer for this one

The timeline is a drag-and-drop chart that needs a bigger screen. On a phone you'll see a note and a button back to the Overview.

2Add phases, tasks and milestones

Start with a + Phase, then add + Task and + Milestone items to it. Every task belongs to a phase.

  • Set the start and end dates, status, priority and how far along it is.
  • Assign a person and the contractor doing the work.
  • Check Critical task if a delay would push out the finish, or Weather delay if weather held it up.
  • Under Dependencies, say what has to happen first (save the task once before adding them).

3Move and update work

Drag a bar to move it, or drag its end to make it longer or shorter. Click any item to open it and change the details. Mark tasks Complete as they finish so the Overview stays right.

Status colors

At risk and Delayed stand out on the chart and feed the project's Needs attention list.

4Lock the plan

Once the schedule is set, click Lock plan. Every task's current dates become the plan. From then on, the Overview and the project card show whether the job is ahead of or behind that plan.

Locking replaces the old plan

If you click Update plan later, today's dates become the new plan and the old comparison is gone. Only do it when the schedule has truly been reset, like after a change order.

The two dates at the top show the Current Scheduled Completion and the Projected Completion.

5Delete an item

Open the item and click Delete. You'll need to type to confirm.

This can't be undone

Deleting a phase or task removes it from the schedule and every number that uses it.

?Questions

Why does the Overview say “plan 44%”?That's how much of the work should be done by today, based on the locked plan. The big number is how much actually is.
What are the dependency types?Finish-to-Start is the usual one: B starts after A finishes. You can also tie starts or finishes together, with a lag in days.
