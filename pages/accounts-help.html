Accounts & accounting

← Back to Accounts

Two tabs on every project keep the money and paperwork straight. Accounts / Contacts tracks each vendor's contract, W-9 and COI. Accounting holds the invoices, draws and budget.

1Vendors on the job

Open Accounts / Contacts. Every vendor working this job is listed, whether they won a bid, were added on their vendor page, or sent an invoice. Vendors who were only invited to bid show up grayed out until they're awarded.

  • Contract: this job only. Missing, waiting on a signature, or signed.
  • W-9: comes from the vendor record, so it's the same on every job.
  • COI (this job): this job's certificate of insurance and when it expires.
  • Cleared for Site: turns to Yes only when the contract, W-9 and COI are all done.

The numbers across the top count what's still missing. Use Everything › Needs attention to show only the vendors who still owe you something.

Check Cleared for Site before a crew shows up

A vendor marked No is missing at least one document. Hover over the tag to see which one.

2Contracts and COIs

Click a vendor's row to open their paperwork. From there you can:

  • Generate contract: builds Leeward's subcontract from the project and the awarded bid. It goes to the Vendor Portal, the vendor initials and signs it, and then the project's Project Manager signs for Leeward.
  • Upload a contract you already have, or one that's already signed.
  • Mark the contract Not needed for this job.
  • View or download the W-9 and this job's COI. For a vendor who won a bid, Request COI emails them to upload one.
Only the PM signs for Leeward

Generated contracts are countersigned by whoever is set as this project's Project Manager. Until then the contract shows Waiting on PM.

3Utility accounts

The Utility Accounts tab keeps the job's water, sewer, electric, trash and similar accounts in one place: provider, account number, phone, service address, meter number, and when the account started or was closed. Click + Add Utility Account to add one.

4Invoices

Open Accounting › Invoices to see every invoice on the job. Filter by Not paid or Paid, or search by vendor or invoice number. Click a file name to open the PDF.

Click + Add invoice, pick the vendor, and fill in the invoice number, date and total. You can attach a PDF or snap photos of a paper invoice; photos are combined into one PDF. The file is named for you and saved in 07 Financial › Invoices.

On the job site?

The camera button on a project's Overview (on your phone) can add an invoice from a photo too.

5Draws and the budget

Accounting, Super Admins and the project's PM also see Draws and Budget.

  • Budget: every budget line by group, with the equity and loan split and retainage. You can import it from the AIA workbook or add lines by hand.
  • Draws: each monthly draw. Open one to see the payment due, add this period's amounts, and give every invoice a budget line. Amounts save as you type.
Give every invoice a budget line

Invoices without a budget line aren't counted in a draw. The Overview's Needs attention list tells you when some are missing.

?Questions

Why is a vendor on this list?They were awarded a bid, added to the project on their vendor page (Manage Projects), sent an invoice on this job, or were invited to bid.
Where does the W-9 come from?From the vendor's record. Upload it once on the Vendors page and it counts on every job.
I don't see Draws or Budget.They're only for Accounting, Super Admins and the project's Project Manager.
Who can edit or delete invoices?That's set per project. If the buttons are missing, ask the project's PM.
