Vendor Information is the company-wide list of every subcontractor and supplier. Keep each vendor's details, W-9, COI and contacts here once, and every project uses them.

1Find a vendor

Open Vendor Information in the sidebar. Each card shows the address, trade tags, and whether the vendor is approved, with their tax ID, W-9 and COI status.

  • Search by name with Search Vendors.
  • Click View Vendor to open their page.
  • The pencil button edits the vendor.

2What “Approved” means

A vendor is Approved once their record has everything we need. If anything is missing, the card says Not Approved and the vendor's page lists what's left.

  • Legal name and full address.
  • SSN / FID and a W-9 (unless they're W-9 exempt).
  • A COI expiration date that hasn't passed (unless they're COI exempt).
  • At least one tag from every tag category, like a trade and an area.
Only approved vendors can be invited to bid

If you need someone on a bid package, finish their record first.

COIs expire

Cards turn to Expiring soon before the date and Expired after. You can choose who gets reminder emails under More Actions › COI Notifications.

3Add or edit a vendor

Click More Actions › + Add Vendor, or the pencil on a card to edit one. Fill in the legal name, a friendly name, the address, the SSN / FID, tags, and upload the W-9. Set the COI expiration date here; the COI PDF itself is filed per project on the vendor's page.

  • Exempt from COI, W-9 or contract requirements are at the bottom, for vendors who truly don't need them.
  • Manage Tags (under More Actions) adds or renames the tags vendors can be given.
Deleting a vendor removes their W-9 too

Delete Vendor is at the bottom of the edit popup. It can't be undone.

4A vendor's page

Click View Vendor to open everything about one company.

  • Projects lists every job they're on, with this job's COI. Upload a project's COI here, or use Manage Projects to add them to a job.
  • Notes are shared with everyone who can see the vendor.
  • Contacts: add, edit or remove people. A contact with a Vendor Portal login shows Vendor Dashboard User.
  • Save all to Phone Contacts downloads the contacts to your phone.

5The Vendor Portal and applications

Vendors have their own portal where they answer bid invites, upload COIs and W-9s, and sign contracts. New companies can apply there. Their requests show up under More Actions › Vendor Applications.

  • Check the W-9, then Approve & Send Sign-in Link. The company is added to Vendors and gets an email to sign in.
  • Or Reject it, with an optional reason that's included in the email.
See what they see

More Actions › Vendor Portal Preview shows the portal the way a vendor sees it.

?Questions

Why is a vendor Not Approved when everything looks done?Open their page. Under Approval it lists exactly what's missing, often a tag from one of the categories.
Is the COI per project or per vendor?Both. The expiration date lives on the vendor record and keeps them approved; the COI PDF is filed for each project.
Can I get a list of vendors by trade?Yes. More Actions › Generate A Report makes a report by trade or by approval status.
