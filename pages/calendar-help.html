Your calendar

The Calendar puts your meetings, tasks, project deadlines and COI expirations in one place, and can show your Google calendar next to them.

1Find your way 2Create an event 3Google & Apple 4Settings ?Questions

1Find your way around

Switch between Day, Week, Month and Agenda at the top. Use the arrows or the small calendar on the left to move around, and Today to jump back.

Turn calendars on and off under My calendars:

  • My events and Assigned to me.
  • Tasks: your own tasks and project to-dos with a due date.
  • Project deadlines: timeline tasks and milestones that end that day.
  • COI expirations, in red, for every vendor.
Overdue moves to today

A task or deadline that's past due and not done shows on today, marked Overdue, so it doesn't get lost.

2Create an event

Click + Create, or click an empty time slot (double-click a day in Month view). Add a title, time, location and notes, and set it to repeat if you need to.

  • Invite people when you want an answer. They can accept or decline, and it shows dashed on their calendar until they do.
  • Assign it when you just want it on someone's calendar, no answer needed.
  • Find a time that works checks everyone's calendar and working hours.

To move an event, drag it to a new day or time.

3Google and Apple calendars

There are two ways to connect, and you can do both:

  • Bring in your Google calendar: paste your Google calendar's secret iCal address. Your Google events show in the portal, read-only.
  • Show the portal calendar in Google or Apple: make your private link and add it to Google or Apple Calendar. Portal events then show on your phone's calendar.
Keep your private link private

Anyone with the link can see your portal calendar. If it gets shared by mistake, make a new link in Calendar settings; the old one stops working.

Updates aren't instant

Portal events show up in Google within a few hours and in Apple within about an hour. Change Google events in Google; the portal updates from there.

4Settings

The gear button opens Calendar settings: your working hours, the day your week starts, colors, phone reminders, the morning summary, and a COI heads-up a few days before a vendor's COI expires. Changes save on their own.

The ? button opens the setup checklist again any time.

?Questions

Why is an event dashed?It's an invite you haven't answered yet. Open it to accept or decline.
Can I edit a Google event here?No. Google events are read-only in the portal. Change them in Google and they'll update here.
Why don't I get reminders on my phone?Turn on phone notifications first (the setup checklist walks you through it). On iPhone the portal has to be added to your Home Screen.
