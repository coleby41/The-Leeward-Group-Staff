To-dos and tasks

← Back to To-do

There are two kinds of to-dos. Each project has its own To-do list for the team, and your personal To-Do page pulls together everything assigned to you.

1Project to-do 2My To-Do 3Delete ?Questions

1A project's to-do list

Open a project and click To-do. Type in + New task and press Enter to add a big item, like Framing inspection, Bldg 2.

Click an item's name to open its checklist on the side. Each checklist row can have its own due date and the person responsible.

Checking off the big item

The big item has its own checkbox. Tick it when the whole thing is done, even if you didn't use every checklist row.

2Your To-Do page

Your personal To-Do page (open it from your tasks on the Home page) has two parts:

  • My Task: your own tasks. Type a task, pick a due date, and press Enter.
  • Project To-Do: every open checklist row assigned to you, on every project, grouped by project.

Checking something off here checks it off on the project too.

They show on your calendar

Tasks and to-dos with a due date show on your Calendar under Tasks, and on the Home page.

3Delete an item

Open the item and use Delete item. You'll be asked to confirm.

Deleting removes the whole checklist

Every checklist row under that item goes with it, and it can't be undone. If the work is just finished, check it off instead.

Someone assigned me something. Where do I see it?On your To-Do page under Project To-Do, on your Calendar, and on the Home page.
What's the difference between the timeline and the to-do list?The timeline is the schedule of the work itself. The to-do list is for the team's own follow-ups, like ordering windows or calling the inspector.
