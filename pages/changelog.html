Release Notes

Welcome to the Leeward Staff Portal. This is everything you can do here, in one place.

Version 1

October 31, 2026 · First release

Getting started

  • Sign in with the username and password IT gave you. Check Remember this device so you stay signed in.
  • Good Morning: the first time you open the portal each day, you get a quick summary of what changed since your last visit.
  • Notifications: the bell in the top right shows anything that needs you. Change Password and Sign out are in the same menu.
  • The sidebar on the left takes you everywhere. You only see the pages your role has access to.

Dashboard

  • Your home base: My Tasks, Quick Links, Weather, a Project Snapshot, Today’s Calendar, Important Contacts, and company Announcements.
  • To-Do: every task assigned to you, from you and from your projects, in one list.

Projects

  • Project Overview lists every project. Start a new one with the New Project Onboarding wizard.
  • Each project has its own pages: Overview, Files, Timeline (schedule and milestones), To-Do, Accounts / Contacts, Form Logs, Accounting, and Bid Invites.
  • Files open right inside the portal. No downloading just to take a look.
  • Invoices: add them on the project’s Accounting page, or snap a photo of one from your phone. Hardware-store receipts work too.
  • Bid Invites: invite approved vendors to bid on a project. Save an invite as a draft and finish it later, and vendors get reminder emails automatically.
  • Photos: take project photos straight from your phone on the project’s Overview.

Vendors

  • Vendor Information is the company-wide vendor list: contacts, addresses, W-9s (or W-9 exempt), and COIs.
  • Add a vendor to a project and request their COI by email, right from the vendor’s page.
  • Review new vendor applications. If the vendor is already on file, merge them instead of creating a duplicate.
  • Vendors get their own Vendor Portal login. A gold Portal Member pill shows which vendors are signed up.

Forms & reports

  • Incident Report: report an issue on any Leeward property from your computer or phone. Approved reports can generate Back Charge and VPO documents for the project.
  • Form Templates: fill out company forms right in the portal. No downloads needed.
  • Excel Workbook Templates: preview and download the latest company workbooks.
  • Account Activity: track every form and incident report you’ve submitted and where it stands.

Time & pay

  • Staff Finance: fill out and submit your timesheet each pay period.
  • Managers review and approve their team’s timesheets in Manage Employees. Accounting runs payroll from Payroll Tools.

On your phone

  • The portal is built for phones too, with a bottom tab bar you can customize and a More menu for everything else.
  • Install it like an app: tap Get the app on the Dashboard and follow the steps to add it to your home screen.

Need help?

  • Found something that isn’t working? Click Report Bug at the bottom of the sidebar and tell us what happened.
  • Can’t see a page you need? Ask IT to check your workgroup’s access.
